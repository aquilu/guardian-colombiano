## Abstract (≈ 495 words; límite 500)

**Guardián colombiano: safety-preserving fine-tuning of Tiny Aya for Colombian-Spanish moderation.**

*12-cuentos* (12cuentos.vasmi.co) is a live public mural of anonymous Colombian collective memory, promoted by a library network and read by
families, children and older adults. Every note passes a closed three-layer guardian: local rules, OpenAI
omni-moderation and gpt-5.4-mini. It works, but it over-blocks colloquial Colombian Spanish (*berraco*, *juepucha*,
rural slang, memories of the armed conflict) and sends victims' memories to external APIs.

We replace it with **Tiny Aya Global (3.35B)**, LoRA-fine-tuned and running in 4-bit on a 6 GB consumer GPU, and
treat safety as a first-class metric.

**Pipeline (chaining Lexsi repos):**
- **CuratorKIT** curates 2,362 synthetic bilingual (ES/EN) notes generated by a GPT-5.4 teacher. They cover 13 policy
  categories, idiom traps, PII obfuscation, prompt injections and code-mixing.
- **SafeTune** trains the same guardian three ways (PlainSFT, Lisa and SafeGrad) on identical data and schedule, with
  352 alignment pairs.
- **AuditKIT** red-teams the 4 frozen models with its built-in probes, plus a Spanish extension we wrote. On prompt
  injection, Lisa resists 5/8 versus 2–3/8 for the others.

**Evaluation built to be trusted:**
- An independent holdout generated by another model family (DeepSeek V4.1).
- A contamination audit against 8 sources.
- Two judges from different families (GPT-5.4 and Claude Sonnet 5.5), plus human committee review of 100 items.
- Frozen with SHA-256 and used exactly once, with paired cluster-bootstrap 95 % CIs.

**Results (ES / EN):**
- **All three adapters fix the task.** The share of unsafe notes that slip through drops from 46/42 % to 5–10 %, and
  the exact JSON schema rate goes from 0–7 % to 92–100 %.
- **Only Lisa also preserves general safety:**
  - its attack success rate on harmful requests goes from 50/59 % (base) and 47/38 % (PlainSFT) to **0/0 %** under
    GPT-5.4;
  - under Claude it is also 0 %, and every difference is significant;
  - its over-refusal of benign requests is 3–6 %;
  - it has the best cross-lingual consistency: 96 % of notes get the same verdict in ES and EN.
- **SafeGrad got worse in Spanish** in our setup (+11.8 points ASR versus base). Our interpretation: SafeGrad
  *protects* an existing alignment direction, while Lisa *installs* refusals.

- **Robust across bases:** the same Lisa recipe on Tiny Aya water and fire also reaches 0/0 % ASR. Regional focus
  barely changes the task.

**ROI:** versus the closed production chain on the same holdout:
- Lisa reaches equal accuracy with 18 points less over-blocking of approved Colombian Spanish;
- it is 6.3× cheaper on a pay-per-use cloud T4, and almost free on the library's own GPU;
- no note leaves the server.

**Upstream contributions:** 5 issues filed with reproductions and fixes, including a fp16 AMP bug that silently divides
SafeGrad's alignment term by the loss scale (SafeTune#37). The others: SafeTune#38–39, aligntune#54 and
CuratorKIT#42.

**Artifacts:** adapters, datasets, the frozen holdout and logs (harmful outputs redacted) on Hugging Face, a live
ZeroGPU demo, and a reproducible public GitHub repo.
